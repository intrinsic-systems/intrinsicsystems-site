# External Preview v1 — public narrative and content audit

Target: 12 September 2026. Status: implementation baseline, subject to founder review.

## Public narrative

Intrinsic Systems is the public parent identity. It is developing an organisational intelligence framework that helps organisations understand their present condition, determine what good means in their own context, and continually strengthen capability towards it. The framework connects knowledge and evidence, makes uncertainty visible, complements authoritative enterprise systems, and preserves human accountability.

The public explanation follows: fragmented knowledge and decaying reasoning → connected and evidence-aware understanding → organisational capability → more confident decisions and improvement.

## Minimum architecture

- Home: problem, proposition, value pathway and first call to action.
- Framework: ECF → Runtime Intelligence → Enterprise Intelligence, connected capability view, controlled vocabulary and confidence discipline.
- About: company purpose, principles and initial operating context.
- Contact: one direct path for bounded use cases, partnerships and support.

## Visual system

The site uses one restrained visual grammar: connected nodes, concentric fields, governed layers and directional loops. Navy expresses foundation and authority; blue expresses active intelligence; sky expresses connection and learning; gold is reserved for qualification or attention. DM Sans carries body copy and Manrope carries headings. Graphics are code-native explanatory components, not decorative illustrations, and reuse the same geometry, line weight, spacing and labels across pages.

## Public language

Public copy begins with familiar language and introduces technical terms only where they add meaning. It speaks directly and positively, avoids defensive “this, not that” constructions, and limits stacked abstract nouns. Canonical distinctions remain intact, but are explained through practical ideas: bringing the picture together, seeing what supports a conclusion, keeping understanding current, and leaving decisions with accountable people.
