# External Preview v1 — public narrative and content audit

Target: 12 September 2026. Status: implementation baseline, subject to founder review.

## Public narrative

Intrinsic Systems is the company. OASIS is its principal platform. OASIS helps organisations connect knowledge and evidence, make uncertainty visible and carry better understanding into decisions, capability and sustained improvement. It complements authoritative enterprise systems and preserves human accountability.

The public explanation follows: fragmented knowledge and decaying reasoning → connected and evidence-aware understanding → organisational capability → more confident decisions and improvement.

## Minimum architecture

- Home: problem, proposition, value pathway and first call to action.
- OASIS: ECF → Runtime Intelligence → Enterprise Intelligence, confidence discipline and current direction.
- About: company purpose, principles and initial operating context.
- Contact: one direct path for bounded use cases, partnerships and support.

## Visual system

The site uses one restrained visual grammar: connected nodes, concentric fields, governed layers and directional loops. Navy expresses foundation and authority; blue expresses active intelligence; sky expresses connection and learning; gold is reserved for qualification or attention. DM Sans carries body copy and Manrope carries headings. Graphics are code-native explanatory components, not decorative illustrations, and reuse the same geometry, line weight, spacing and labels across pages.
